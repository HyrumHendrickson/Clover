# Code of conduct

We want Clover to be a respectful, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with differences in experience and perspective.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, threats, or publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation for behaviour that breaks this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting or by contacting a repository owner. Reports are handled with discretion.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public. It is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
